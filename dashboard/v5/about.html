What is SugarTrack?

SugarTrack is a comprehensive, privacy-first web application engineered to simplify blood glucose monitoring. Managing diabetes or tracking metabolic health shouldn't require clunky spreadsheets or outdated software. We built SugarTrack to bridge the gap between medical utility and modern software design.

By combining an intuitive, flat "Square UI" aesthetic with powerful backend cloud syncing via Google Firebase, SugarTrack ensures your data is instantly accessible, beautifully visualized, and securely stored across all your devices.

Our Mission

We believe that personal health tools should be as beautifully designed and fluid as the apps you use for entertainment. Our mission is to reduce the friction of daily medical logging through intelligent design, empowering users to make better data-driven decisions about their health.

Platform Modules

The Dashboard

A high-level overview of your daily health. Features an intelligent time-weighted 7-day average, dynamic trend indicators, and a beautiful Chart.js visualization of your most recent readings.

The Logbook

Your complete historical database. Effortlessly log new readings with contextual tags (Fasting, Post-Meal, Bedtime) and optional notes to track carbohydrates or insulin doses.

System Inbox

A native-feeling split-pane messaging center. Receive system alerts, doctor communications, and health insights directly within the app.

Customization

Fully personalize your experience with dynamic Light/Dark modes, custom accent color injection, and customizable user avatars.
